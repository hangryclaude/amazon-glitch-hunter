# Glitch 17 — First Reads, Prime Reading & free Kindle books 🟢

**Category:** Free digital goods (Prime)
**Effort:** Zero — click once a month
**Risk:** None.

## How it works

Prime membership includes a permanent free-book pipeline:

- **Amazon First Reads** — every month, editors pick ~10 pre-release
  Kindle books. Prime members pick **one (often two) FREE** before
  publication; non-Prime pays $1.99 each. Books are yours to keep
  forever. Select months add a free bonus short read.
- **Prime Reading** — a rotating catalog of free ebooks, magazines,
  comics, and some audiobooks, borrowable anytime.
- Free **Kindle First** is the old name of the same First Reads benefit.

## How to work it

- Visit amazon.com/firstreads on the **1st of every month** — the new
  picks drop then and never repeat.
- Two-book months happen several times a year (October 2026 gave two).
- Keep old No-Rush digital credits (Glitch 11) to buy books *outside*
  the free picks for free.
