# Glitch 11 — No-Rush shipping credits ("free money") 🟢/🟡

**Category:** Prime perk
**Effort:** Zero — just click a different button at checkout
**Risk:** None. Amazon's own offer. (Reward value now variable — see update below.)

## How it works

> **2026 update (verified Oct 2026):** the guaranteed **$1 digital credit is
> no longer consistent** — the perk is still live but frequently replaced by
> instant order discounts, account-targeted offers, or ~1% delayed-shipping
> rewards with Amazon-branded cards. Expect the *perk*, not the fixed dollar.

Prime members at checkout can choose **FREE No-Rush Shipping** instead of
fast shipping. In exchange, Amazon credits your account with a **promotional
reward** — typically **$1 digital credit** per order (sometimes more during
Prime Day or special promos, occasionally instant discounts on the current
order instead).

## How to stack it

- Choose No-Rush on **every order you don't need immediately**. Credits add up.
- Rewards post when the package **ships**; check the email for redemption rules.
- Credits spend on select **digital products**: Kindle ebooks, digital music,
  instant video rentals/downloads, Appstore apps. Kindle ebooks are the classic use.
- Watch for **double points days** (community-reported promos where the credit multiplies).
- Related free-credit vectors, all 🟢:
  - **"Get $5 when you add a backup card"** wallet promos
  - **Amazon Assistant / receipt-scan promos** (upload receipts from other
    retailers for credit)
  - **Prime Day/Audible signup promos** (e.g. 3 free months Audible + $20
    audiobook credit for new/returning subscribers, Prime Day 2026)

## Notes

- Rewards **expire** — check balance: Account → "Your No-Rush rewards" /
  promotional balance.
- Availability of the option fluctuates; some users report it disappearing
  for stretches except during Prime events.
