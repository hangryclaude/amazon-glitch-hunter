# Glitch 10 — Late delivery compensation credits 🟢

**Category:** Prime perk / service guarantee
**Effort:** Low — a chat message when a package is late
**Risk:** None if truthful. (Faking lateness = 🟠 deception.)

## How it works

Prime promises delivery dates. When Amazon misses them, customers can claim
compensation. Typical payouts reported:

- **$5–$10 promotional credit** (most common now)
- historically, **a free month of Prime** (rarer these days)
- refund of **shipping fees** on guaranteed-delivery orders (official policy)

Amazon has become stingier over time, but the flow is standardized:

1. Log in → **Your Orders** → find the late order.
2. **Problem with order** → **Shipping or delivery issues** → **Shipment is late**.
3. Connect with support; state the order had a **guaranteed delivery date**,
   the promised date, and that it arrived late (or hasn't arrived).
4. **Politely ask what compensation is available.** Being straightforward and
   factual works best.

## Pro tips

- Reference the **delivery guarantee** explicitly — guaranteed-delivery
  orders qualify for a **shipping fee refund** by policy even when nothing else
  is offered.
- Prime members with no shipping fee to refund usually get promo credits instead.
- Support outcomes are **subjective** — different channels/reps sometimes
  yield different compensation. Multiple polite contacts can pay off.
- Compensation claims must generally be made **within 30 days** of the
  estimated delivery date.
