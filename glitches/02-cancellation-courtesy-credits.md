# Glitch 02 — Cancelled price-error orders → courtesy gift card credit 🟡

**Category:** Price glitch byproduct
**Effort:** Low — just order glitch deals and wait
**Risk:** None. This is Amazon policy, not a hack.

## How it works

When Amazon lists an item at a wrong price and you order it, they often
**cancel the order**. Standard policy sometimes adds a **courtesy gift card
credit** to your account for the inconvenience. It doesn't happen every time,
but it happens often enough that deal hunters watch for price errors
specifically to collect the cancellation credits.

## How to work it

1. Find price-error listings (see [01-price-glitches.md](01-price-glitches.md)
   for hunting methods — timing matters because listings get fixed fast).
2. Order the glitch-priced item(s).
3. If Amazon cancels, check your account for a courtesy credit
   (Account → Gift cards / check email).
4. The credit is spendable like cash on eligible purchases.

## Notes

- The glitch-hunting community (e.g. groups that post "price errors +
  cancellations scripts") treats the courtesy credit as the **second prize**
  when the glitch-price order itself doesn't get honored.
- Refunds/credits to **gift card balance** process much faster (hours) than
  refunds to credit/debit cards (3–10 days) — a general Amazon fact worth
  exploiting: when given the choice, take credits over card refunds.
