# Glitch 20 — Amazon Outlet, Warehouse & Woot 🟢/🟡

**Category:** Deep-discount channels
**Effort:** Medium — browse three sites habitually
**Risk:** None for Outlet/Woot; Warehouse condition risk 🟡-ish (described honestly below).

## How it works

Amazon owns three legitimate clearance channels:

- **Amazon Outlet** — official overstock & clearance; new items at
  "overstock prices," often 40–70% off.
- **Amazon Warehouse** — returned/open-box items graded *Used – Like New,
  Very Good, Good, Acceptable* with steep discounts. Because these are
  single-stock items, **mispriced/oddly-cheap units appear and Amazon
  honors the listed price you paid** — the glitch-hunter tool (README)
  catches these. Europe now runs "Second Chance Deal Days" discount
  events around them.
- **Woot** (woot.com) — Amazon-owned daily-deal site since 2010. Daily
  limited-stock sales up to ~89% off, plus the legendary **Bag of
  Crap** (BoC): a few dollars of random warehouse junk that occasionally
  contains a big-ticket surprise.

## How to work it

- Warehouse: sort by discount within a category; condition-graded items
  are one-of-one — buy the anomalous price immediately.
- Woot: check woot.com daily; BoC appears randomly during Woot-Offs and
  sells out in seconds (join Woot's sellout alerts).
- Prime members get free shipping on Woot.
