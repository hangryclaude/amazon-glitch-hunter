# Glitch 16 — Gift-card bonus credits & Prime-event credit earning 🟢

**Category:** Promo credit
**Effort:** Low — buy a gift card, do a task, get credit
**Risk:** None. Amazon's own offers.

## How it works

Two recurring credit machines:

1. **"Buy $50 gift card, get $5"** — Amazon repeatedly runs promos where
   buying (or reloading) $50+ in Amazon gift cards earns a $5 promotional
   credit. It recurs around Prime Day and holidays; sometimes drops to a
   $25 buy-in. You're buying money at a ~10% discount.
2. **Event credit-earning actions** — during Prime Day / Big Deal Days,
   Amazon pays credits for small tasks: streaming on Prime Video
   ($5), downloading the Amazon app ($5 off $10), buying via Affirm ($25),
   and grocery actions like **$5 credit per $25 Amazon Now order, up to
   $25 total**. The "12 Weeks of Prime" program formalizes this for
   new/returning members.

## How to work it

- Each promo is usually **once per account**, but they recur every event
  — check the Prime Day landing pages each July and October.
- Gift-card credit counts as payment on (almost) anything; combine with
  coupons (Glitch 03).
- Task credits stack with each other: do every task on the event page.
