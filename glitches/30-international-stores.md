# Glitch 30 — International storefronts 🟢/🟡

**Category:** Glitch hunting on non-US Amazon marketplaces
**Effort:** Medium — accounts, shipping math, customs rules per country
**Risk:** Low. Same rules as US glitches, plus import fees.

## 1. Why non-US storefronts glitch too 🟢

Every Amazon marketplace (.co.uk, .de, .co.jp, .ca, .com.au, .fr, .it, .es, .in,
.mx, .com.br) has its own listings, sellers, and repricers — so each one produces
its own price errors. The UK's 2014 "1p glitch" (third-party repricer error put
thousands of items at £0.01) and 2021 air-fryer glitches (£44 → £3.99) are the
classics. Big glitches hit the US first because that's where the deal forums
are — regional stores get less hunter traffic, so errors live longer.

- UK 1p glitch history: https://goaura.com/blog/amazon-glitch
- 2021 UK £3.99 air fryer: https://www.thesun.co.uk/money/15919678/amazon-glitch-reduced-air-fryer-from-48-to-4/
- Another UK glitch example: https://www.lancs.live/news/uk-world-news/amazon-glitch-slashes-listed-air-21360148
- UK/EU glitch groups exist (House of Resell): https://whop.com/blog/amazon-glitch-deals

## 2. Regional-only promos & credits 🟢

Promo credits and "add $X get $Y" offers are launched per-marketplace and often
never appear in the US. Amazon.co.jp gives Prime points and has Japan-only Prime
benefits; .de runs EU-only coupon events. Use a VPN to browse other storefronts
(see [VPN-GUIDE.md](../VPN-GUIDE.md)) and check each country's Today's Deals
page. Note: Prime membership does NOT transfer — you'd need an account (and
usually a local address/payment) per storefront.

- Japan-only Prime benefits list: https://www.amazon.co.jp/-/en/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G6LDPN7YJHYKH2J6
- Prime benefits differ by country (catalog, shipping): https://redstagfulfillment.com/how-many-countries-offer-amazon-prime
- Reddit: cheaper UK listings hidden behind pricier EU offers: https://www.reddit.com/r/AmazonUK/comments/1qmkznd/discovered_glitch_amazon_uk_lower_priced_products/

## 3. Importing a glitch deal ⚠️

Buying from a foreign storefront to your home country = you are the importer of
record. Amazon Global Store collects an estimated Import Tax & Fees deposit at
checkout (calculated on the discounted price, plus shipping) and settles with
customs for you. A $5 glitch laptop can still carry $20+ of import fees. Some
countries have de-minimis thresholds below which nothing is charged. Also note
the Prime Visa 5% only earns on your home storefront, not international Amazon
sites.

- Import Tax & Fees rules: https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G8VRJ7Y8Z3T5WPV3
- Customs, duties & taxes: https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G26L6NHEDGERVR8W
- Cross-country Amazon buying tips: https://wise.com/us/blog/buying-from-amazon-in-a-different-country
- Prime Visa excludes international Amazon sites: https://www.amazon.com/b?ie=UTF8&node=16070413011

## 4. The cross-marketplace arbitrage angle 🟡

Keepa tracks 11 marketplaces, so a US price-glitch pattern (95%+ off, sudden
rank spike) can be hunted per-country on the same product. Rare-language
marketplaces (.co.jp, .com.br) see the least hunter competition. Orders usually
need a local delivery address — freight forwarders work but reintroduce customs
fees.
