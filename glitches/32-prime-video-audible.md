# Glitch 32 — Prime Video / Audible / Music freebies 🟢

**Category:** Digital freebies included in (or bundled with) Prime
**Effort:** Low — sign up / claim, remember to cancel trials
**Risk:** None if you cancel trials before they bill.

## 1. Audible trials — 2 credits for Prime members 🟢

New Audible customers who are Prime members get **2 free audiobook credits**
(standard trial is 1) — each good for any bestseller, kept forever even after
cancelling. Returning members get the standard 1-credit trial. Trials have
repeatedly come bundled with extra Amazon credits (e.g. $15 Prime Now credit
historically). Check the Prime-member trial banner when logged in.

- Prime-member double trial (official): https://www.audible.ca/ep/free-trial-amazon-prime
- Trial + $15 credit bundle (historic example): https://www.mysavings.com/free-samples/audible-prime-now/109193

## 2. Audible Plus catalog & Prime Reading audio 🟢

Audible's Plus Catalog (thousands of audiobooks, Originals, podcasts) is
included during any trial and with cheap membership tiers — effectively free
listening during the trial month. Prime Reading already includes a rotating
set of audiobooks at $0 for Prime members (see [Glitch 17](17-first-reads-kindle.md)).

- Plus Catalog details: https://www.audible.ca/ep/free-trial-amazon-prime
- Prime Reading (JP page shows the pattern): https://www.amazon.co.jp/-/en/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G6LDPN7YJHYKH2J6

## 3. Prime Music freebies people miss 🟢

Prime members get **Amazon Music Prime** free — ad-free shuffle of ~100 million
songs (Japan page confirms 100M; catalog varies by market) — plus recurring
"1–3 months of Music Unlimited free" promos for Prime members. The Unlimited
trials are the credit-worthy part: claim, download, cancel.

- Music Prime included: https://www.amazon.co.jp/-/en/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G6LDPN7YJHYKH2J6
- Free month promos roundup: https://www.freestufffinder.com/amazon-freebies/

## 4. Prime Video included channels & FAST channels 🟢

Beyond the "Included with Prime" catalog: Amazon has folded free ad-supported
(FAST) channels into Prime Video — e.g. Rakuten TV's channels rolled out free
on Prime Video across Germany, Spain, Italy in 2026, and similar free
channels (Crime+Investigation, Spiegel TV) have launched per-region. Paid
Channel subscriptions (Max, Paramount+ etc.) frequently run free-preview
windows and Prime-member discounts. Anime/foreign storefronts carry
region-exclusive included content.

- Rakuten FAST channels free on Prime Video: https://www.broadbandtvnews.com/2026/03/02/rakuten-tv-rolls-out-fast-channels-on-amazon-prime-video-in-europe
- Channel example + 14-day free trials: https://www.broadbandtvnews.com/2020/06/04/crime-investigation-play-launches-on-amazon-prime-video-channels
- Catalog varies by country: https://redstagfulfillment.com/how-many-countries-offer-amazon-prime

## 5. Annual event credits 🟢

Prime Day / Big Deal Days and Black Friday cycle predictable digital promos:
Audible 2-credit trials, Music Unlimited free months, Prime Gaming drops
([Glitch 18](18-prime-gaming.md)), and $5–10 promo credits for watching
trailer/pilot content or using Alexa ([Glitch 22](22-alexa-voice-promos.md)).
Calendar them — they're 🟢 and repeat every year.
