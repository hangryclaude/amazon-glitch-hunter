# Glitch 24 — "Free product for review" seller groups 🟠

**Category:** Third-party seller ecosystem (Facebook/Telegram/Discord)
**Effort:** Medium — find groups, front the money, wait for rebates
**Risk:** Real — account flags, lost money if seller ghosts you

## How it works

Amazon sellers need early reviews to rank. Since the 2016 ban on
*incentivized* reviews, they route it through private groups: Facebook
groups, Telegram/Discord channels, and rebate sites (RebateKey etc.).
The flow:

1. Seller posts an offer in the group (product, ASIN, keywords).
2. You buy the item **at full price on Amazon** like a normal customer.
3. You leave a 5-star review, often including specified **keywords**
   (hence "keyword review groups") — sometimes with photo/video.
4. Seller refunds you via **PayPal, a card rebate, or an Amazon "review
   refund"** — seller issues a partial/full refund on the order (e.g.
   "wrong item sent" or goodwill) so the product is free but the review
   stays verified-purchase.

Amazon has sued/blocked 10,000+ of these Facebook groups; they just
re-form under new names.

## ToS risk for buyers 🟠

- Amazon's Customer Product Reviews policy explicitly prohibits reviews tied
  to free/discounted products arranged through third-party review clubs.
  Detection = review wipes at minimum; account suspension possible.
- Amazon's own sting operations analyzed buyer accounts to sue group admins.
- Seller-side risk too: a refund in exchange for review can get the *seller*
  suspended, and some "refund" offers are outright scams (FTC has warned
  about fake refund texts, and buyers report sellers who never pay).

## How to work it

Only if you'd accept the account risk: 🟠 at best. The fully-legal
equivalent is **Amazon Vine** (Glitch 13) — same free products, zero ToS
risk, but invitation-only.
