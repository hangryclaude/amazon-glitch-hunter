# Glitch 33 — Misgraded Warehouse / used items 🟢/🟡/🟠

**Category:** Amazon Warehouse (Amazon Resale) grading slop
**Effort:** Low — buy, inspect, claim when warranted
**Risk:** Low if honest; 🟠 if you claim damage that isn't real.

## 1. "Acceptable" often arrives new 🟢

Amazon barely inspects returns — items get a Warehouse grade and a price cut, and
"Used – Acceptable" frequently means nothing more than damaged packaging; the
item itself is untouched. Buyers routinely report Acceptable items arriving
new-in-shrinkwrap. The play: sort Warehouse listings by discount, buy the
ugliest grade on a high-value item, and pocket 30–70% off. "Like New" is
often literally new (open-box). Books routinely hit 80%+ off.

- Grading scale (official): https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202074290
- "Amazon basically doesn't inspect returns": https://www.reddit.com/r/YouShouldKnow/comments/s3v7te/ysk_amazon_warehouses_description_of_used/
- Acceptable = usually packaging damage: https://www.garagejournal.com/forum/threads/amazon-warehouse-condition-used-acceptable.442100
- Grading definitions + typical discounts: https://www.deepwaterhappy.com/2020/12/17/what-does-used-acceptable-mean-in-the-amazon-warehouse-up-to-70-off-books-up-to-87-off

## 2. "Item not as described" on grading 🟡

The grade is Amazon's promise. If your "Very Good" arrives with the exact
scratches/missing accessories that "Acceptable" would allow, that's a
condition mismatch — return for free, or chat for a partial refund/returnless
resolution. Sellers see this as the #1 grading risk; Amazon weighs the listing
condition notes against what arrived. Only claim when the item is genuinely
worse than graded — inventing damage drifts to 🟠.

- Condition claims as a seller-side risk: https://goaura.com/blog/amazon-used-like-new
- Warehouse grading guide: https://sellersnap.io/amazon-warehouse-deals-guide

## 3. Replace-cheaper-used trick 🟡

The used cousin of return-and-rebuy ([Glitch 12](12-return-and-rebuy.md)):
when the same ASIN appears in Warehouse *after* you bought it new at a higher
price, return the new one (free returns) and rebuy the discounted used/like-new
unit — or ask chat to price-adjust against the Warehouse listing. Grading slop
means you often end up with the same item, cheaper. Works best in the 30-day
return window on items where Warehouse stock fluctuates daily.

- Return-and-rebuy base pattern: https://www.reddit.com/r/amazonprime/comments/1gt2bgk/amazon_resending_returned_used_items_getting_out/
- Amazon Resale conditions page: https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202074290

## 4. The trap: "Used – As Is" 🟢

New-ish "As Is" grade = extensive wear, missing parts, NO returns. Only buy
it when the discount covers the worst case.
