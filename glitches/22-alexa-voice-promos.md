# Glitch 22 — Alexa voice-shopping promos 🟢

**Category:** Voice-shopping perk
**Effort:** Low — ask Alexa instead of the app
**Risk:** None.

## How it works

Amazon pays you to shop by voice (it trains the habit and is cheaper to
serve than the app):

- **First voice-order credit** — Amazon has repeatedly offered a
  **$10 credit on your first Alexa-placed order** (documented by AP-syndicated
  reporting and Amazon's own onboarding promos).
- **Alexa-exclusive deals** — recurring "Voice Shopping Weekend" events
  discount items only when ordered by saying "Alexa, order …".
- **Reorder discounts** — asking Alexa to reorder a past purchase
  sometimes surfaces reorder-only coupons on consumables.

## How to work it

- If you've never ordered by voice, check the Alexa app / Echo onboarding
  screen first — the first-order credit is usually sitting there.
- Order by voice during Prime Day / holiday "voice shopping" events.
- Voice ordering must be enabled in Alexa settings (voice code optional).
