# Glitch 34 — The trophy wall: biggest Amazon glitches ever 🟢

**Category:** Historical archive of famous price errors
**Effort:** None — read for calibration on what's possible
**Risk:** None. Mostly honored or canceled-and-refunded.

One-line each, newest first. Lessons: glitch windows are minutes, big-ticket
items glitch hardest, and Amazon *sometimes* honors it anyway.

## The wall

- **2026 — $550 RTX-5070 gaming PCs.** iBUYPOWER desktops glitched ~90% off; many shipped. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcE81NsyAxk
- **2026 — 12-pack sunscreen for $11, $51 Lacoste sneakers, etc.** price-error accounts (@Pricerrors, 268k followers) now post them in real time. https://x.com/Pricerrors
- **2021 — UK air fryer, £44 → £3.99.** RUXINGGU air fryer glitch on amazon.co.uk; national-press frenzy. https://www.thesun.co.uk/money/15919678/amazon-glitch-reduced-air-fryer-from-48-to-4/
- **~2020s — $900 laptops for $5.** Deal-group members (eMoney) grabbed up to 10 each; confirmed shipped. https://whop.com/blog/amazon-glitch-deals
- **~2020s — 2 oz gold coin, $5,500 → $760.** Profit Lounge members; ~86% off precious metal. https://whop.com/blog/amazon-glitch-deals
- **~2020s — $3,000 Samsung TV + soundbar for $500.** Bandar's Bounties confirmed shipped. https://whop.com/blog/amazon-glitch-deals
- **2019 — Prime Day $94.48 camera glitch.** For ~30 minutes after midnight July 15, nearly every Sony camera, lens, and telescope rang up at exactly $94.48 (a $13,000 lens = 99.3% off). Amazon honored at least some orders — the GOAT. https://www.cbsnews.com/news/some-prime-day-glitch-thousands-of-dollars-worth-of-camera-equipment-sold-for-just-94/ · https://www.cbsnews.com/news/some-prime-day-glitch-thousands-of-dollars-worth-of-camera-equipment-sold-for-just-94/ · https://daily.slickdeals.net/shopping/amazon-price-mistake-best-current-reports
- **2018 — Prime Day launch crash.** Site-wide errors in opening minutes; angry shoppers got apology credits. https://www.cnbc.com/2018/07/16/amazon-suffers-glitches-in-opening-minutes-of-prime-day.html
- **2014 — UK 1p glitch.** Repricer bug listed thousands of marketplace items at £0.01 (Kindles, electronics); Amazon canceled most, some shipped. https://goaura.com/blog/amazon-glitch
- **~2010s — everything for a penny (seller-side glitch).** 1,600+ orders at $0.01 from a seller pricing bug; canceled. https://abc7ny.com/post/amazon-glitch-let-shoppers-buy-anything-for-a-penny/437285

## Takeaways for hunters

1. Most orders get **canceled** — but shipped = yours (see [Glitch 08](08-duplicate-wrong-item.md) and cancellation credits, [Glitch 02](02-cancellation-courtesy-credits.md)).
2. Glitches cluster at **high-load moments**: Prime Day launch, Black Friday, late-night repricer runs.
3. Speed is everything — the $94.48 window was **~30 minutes**. That's why the monitoring playbook ([Glitch 36](36-monitoring-playbook.md)) exists.
