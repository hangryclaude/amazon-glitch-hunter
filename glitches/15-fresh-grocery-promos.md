# Glitch 15 — Amazon Fresh grocery promos & perishable price glitches 🟢/🟡

> **2026 update (verified Oct 2026):** no stable public first-order code
> exists anymore — promos are now mostly targeted or item-scoped (currently:
> free delivery on first Fresh order $50+ with Prime; rotating item promos
> like $10 off $30+ selected grocery). The Prime Access $5-off-$25 monthly
> credit expired Oct 1, 2026. Old codes like `GROCERY10` are not verified current.

**Category:** Grocery promo / price glitch
**Effort:** Low — watch for targeted credits and codes
**Risk:** None. All Amazon-offered.

## How it works

Amazon runs recurring grocery credits that make real food nearly free:
- **First-order codes** for Amazon Fresh newbies (e.g. `GROCERY10` $10 off,
  `FRESH50`, periodic $20-off-$100 account-attached promos).
- **Prime Access monthly grocery credit**: $5 off $25+ everyday-essentials
  grocery orders, auto-applied at checkout (ran July–Sept 2026).
- **Prime-event store coupons**: e.g. $15 off $55+ in Amazon Fresh stores
  during Prime Big Deal Days.
- **Perishable price glitches**: Fresh inventory is repriced often, and
  mistakes happen — documented cases include 2-lb salmon slabs for **$3**
  and eggs/$1 produce finds.

## How to work it

- Check your account banner / Fresh page before any grocery order —
  credits are frequently targeted, not public.
- Stack a first-order code with weekly Fresh coupons.
- Glitch window is short: when a perishable drops 70–90%+, order
  immediately — Amazon fixes it within minutes to hours.
- Graded 🟢 for promos; buying a mispriced glitch is 🟢 (Amazon
  honors the charged price, see Glitch 01).
