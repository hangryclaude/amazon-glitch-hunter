# Glitch 14 — Amazon Household: one Prime, two adults 🟢

**Category:** Prime perk sharing
**Effort:** Low — account setup
**Risk:** None when used per policy (with actual household members).

## How it works

**Amazon Household** lets two adults in the same household share Prime
benefits (shipping, Prime Video, Prime Reading, etc.) plus up to 4 teen /
4 child profiles — **on one Prime membership**. Effectively halves (or more)
the cost of Prime per person. Families also share:
- **Family Library** — share eligible ebooks/apps across adult accounts
- Teens can shop with parent approval

## Gray variant 🟡

Sharing with a *non-household* friend (splitting Prime with someone across
the country) technically violates the Prime terms (Household is meant for
people living together). Amazon rarely polices it aggressively, but it's a
terms-of-service stretch, not law-breaking.
