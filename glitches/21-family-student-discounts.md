# Glitch 21 — Family/Student/government-assistance discounts 🟢

**Category:** Membership discount
**Effort:** One-time signup (verify eligibility)
**Risk:** None — lying about eligibility would be 🟠.

## How it works

Three official steep-discount membership tracks:

- **Prime Student** — 6-month free trial, then 50% off Prime
  (~$7.49/mo) for enrolled students.
- **Prime Access** — Prime for **$6.99/mo** (vs $14.99) for EBT/Medicaid
  and other government-assistance recipients or income-verified
  customers; full Prime benefits included.
- **Amazon Family** (free with Prime) — up to **20% off diapers**
  (subscribe & save + family discount), plus the **Baby Registry
  Completion Discount: 15% off** remaining registry items, valid 60 days
  before to 90 days after the due date.

## How to work it

- Stack Family diaper 20% with regular coupons/S&S promos (Glitch 04).
- Registry completion: hold purchases until the 60-day window opens,
  then apply 15% to everything left (up to the cap).
- Prime Access also unlocks the monthly $5 grocery credit (Glitch 15).
