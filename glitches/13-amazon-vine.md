# Glitch 13 — Amazon Vine: free products, by design 🟢

**Category:** Official free-stuff program
**Effort:** Medium — build a review reputation and wait for an invite
**Risk:** None. It's Amazon's own program. (Taxes on item value may apply.)

## How it works

**Amazon Vine** is Amazon's invitation-only review program. Sellers submit
products; Amazon's invited **Vine Voices** order them **free of charge** and
write honest reviews. This is the single most reliable "free items from
Amazon" pipeline that exists — it's literally the program's purpose.

## How to get in

1. You can't apply — Amazon invites "customers who consistently write
   insightful reviews."
2. So: **write a lot of good reviews.** Detailed, structured, photo/video
   reviews on products you genuinely bought. Review rank matters.
3. Historically an invitation shows up in your account/emails once your review
   record builds trust.
4. Once in: pick from thousands of free products daily (up to a quota —
   currently reported as a rolling item limit, e.g. 8 items/day caps and
   100-item rolling windows), shipped free.

## Rules

- Reviews must be **honest** — positive, neutral, or negative all allowed.
- You keep the products. Reviews carry a "Vine Customer Review of Free Product" badge.
- Vine item value can count as taxable income (Amazon issues tax forms) —
  factor that in.

## Related 🟢 programs

- **Amazon Influencer Program** — storefront/video reviews earn commissions;
  some video-review funnels also yield free products.
- Reviewing without Vine: anyone with an account can review, but only Vine
  gets you the free product pipeline.
