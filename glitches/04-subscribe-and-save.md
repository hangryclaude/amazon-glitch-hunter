# Glitch 04 — Subscribe & Save: the most reliable "sanctioned glitch" 🟢

**Category:** Standing program, exploit-friendly by design
**Effort:** Low
**Risk:** None. Amazon's own help pages allow cancel-anytime.

## The moves

### 1. Subscribe → get first delivery → cancel

The first S&S order locks the discount (5–15%) plus any first-delivery
coupon. **Cancel the subscription immediately after the order ships** (or even
right after placing it) and keep the discounted first delivery. No penalty —
Amazon tolerates it because most customers forget to cancel.

- Documented: $9.50 off $188.99 chlorine tablets, then instant cancellation
  (Rather-Be-Shopping). r/amazon users confirm doing this "all the time."
- Cancel anytime (official): https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GQBVD8QMLXXV5GLV

### 2. The 15% tier hack

5+ items shipping in the same month unlock **15% off every item** in that
delivery. Pad the cart with cheap staples to cross the 5-item line, then trim.

### 3. Re-subscribe for new-subscriber coupons

Canceling a subscription and re-adding can regenerate **"first-time subscriber"
coupons ($5–$10 off)** that returning subscribers don't see.

### 4. Free first delivery (non-Prime)

S&S includes free standard shipping on auto-deliveries — non-Prime members
get the first delivery free.

### 5. Bonus: glitch orders ship more often via S&S

Community lore: **S&S-ordered price glitches ship most often** (fewer pricing
review checkpoints) — always place glitch-price orders through Subscribe &
Save when offered.

## Known quirk

Cancelled S&S items occasionally **reactivate automatically** — check your
subscriptions page after canceling.
(https://www.amazonforum.com/s/question/0D5at00000Uil6rCAB/cancelled-subscribe-and-save-items-reactivating-automatically)
