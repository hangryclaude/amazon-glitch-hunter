# Glitch 27 — Amazon Haul freebies & stacking 🟢

**Category:** Amazon storefront promos
**Effort:** Low — fill a $25 cart
**Risk:** None. All official Haul terms.

## How it works

Amazon Haul (launched Nov 2024) is Amazon's Temu fighter: a separate
ultra-cheap storefront inside the Amazon app/site — everything under $20,
with literal $0.01–$3 sections, slow China-shipped, 15-day returns
(items under $3 are returnless — refund, keep it).

The free/nearly-free stack, straight from Amazon's own Haul terms:

1. **Free shipping at $25+** (below that, a flat ~$5.99 fee).
2. **Tiered auto-discounts:** extra 5% off $50+, 10% off $75+.
3. **$10-off-$25 coupons** periodically pushed to select accounts.
4. Watch for **"extra X% off" basket events** — Haul has run sitewide
   bonus discounts (e.g. extra % during holiday pushes) that stack on the
   tiers.

## How to work it 🟢

- Max the tiers: a $75+ cart after coupons can land at ~$55 effective,
  i.e. ~$0.70/item across ~75 cheap items.
- Check your account for the $10-off-$25 coupon before building a big cart
  — it's targeted, and deal blogs track when it's live.
- Sub-$3 items are **returnless-refund eligible** by default (Glitch 05's
  best category, now systematized).
- No Prime perks apply — don't expect fast shipping or normal returns.

## Verdict

🟢 throughout — it's all published Haul policy. The "glitch" is just that
most people don't know the tiers stack with the targeted coupons and the
sub-$3 returnless rule. Quality is Temu-tier; that's the real price.
