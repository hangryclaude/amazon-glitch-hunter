# Glitch 31 — Amazon Business perks 🟢

**Category:** Free B2B account stacking
**Effort:** One signup with a work email (sole proprietors qualify)
**Risk:** None — all official Amazon Business terms.

## 1. The free tier is real free stuff 🟢

An Amazon Business account costs nothing and unlocks: business-only pricing on
millions of items (often lower than consumer price on the same listing),
quantity discounts, and ATEP tax exemption (auto-applied at checkout — worth
real money if you're tax-exempt). Business accounts also get their own coupons
and events — e.g. "35% off up to $125" new-account promos and Prime Big Deal
Days business deals.

- Free tier + business-only pricing: https://checkthat.ai/brands/amazon-business/pricing
- Current business promo banner (rotates): https://business.amazon.com
- ATEP tax exemption details: https://checkthat.ai/brands/amazon-business/pricing

## 2. Prime Business Duo — free shared-Prime-ish tier 🟢

If you already pay for personal Prime, **Prime Business Duo is free**: link your
business account and get 2% back on Amazon brands, business shipping perks, and
member-exclusive discounts (CrowdStrike 50% off, 70% off Gusto payroll, etc.)
without paying a second membership. Full Business Prime (Essentials/Small/Medium)
adds multi-user shipping, rewards up to 4% back, and 45-day payment terms —
paid, so only worth it for actual businesses. A household or side hustle can
stack the personal Prime freebies and the Duo business perks simultaneously.

- Duo free for Prime members: https://business.amazon.com/en/blog/small-business-benefits-of-prime-business
- Plans & benefits: https://business.amazon.com/en/prime-business
- Duo tier details: https://www.business.com/articles/amazon-business-features
- What is Prime Business: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-business-prime

## 3. Business-only events & credits 🟢

Prime Day has a parallel Amazon Business track with extra rewards, and business
accounts get targeted credits/coupons via email. Amazon runs procurement
integrations (Punchout, e-procurement) with event-specific deals. One caveat:
deals expire if the PO arrives late.

- Prime Day 2026 business deals + rewards: https://business.amazon.com/en/blog/prime-day-2026
- Procurement deal timing rules: https://business.amazon.com/en/blog/procurement-integrations-prime-day-prep

## 4. "GRAVITY" promos — mostly myth ⚠️

No verifiable Amazon Business "GRAVITY" promotion exists as of Oct 2026; the
real procurement-flavored perks are the ones above (Duo rewards, member
exclusives like discounted QuickBooks/CrowdStrike, event deals). Don't buy a
"GRAVITY code" from anyone.

- Member exclusives (what actually exists): https://business.amazon.com/en/prime-business
