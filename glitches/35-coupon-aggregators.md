# Glitch 35 — Coupon & deal-aggregator workflow 🟢

**Category:** Passive deal-finding stack
**Effort:** One-time setup of sites, extensions, alerts
**Risk:** None — all sanctioned tools.

## 1. Amazon's own coupon surface 🟢

Amazon's Coupons page (amazon.com/coupons) lists every active clip coupon,
filterable by category; stack a clip coupon + Lightning Deal + Subscribe & Save
and you approach [Glitch 03](03-coupon-stacking.md) territory. Also check
Today's Deals, Outlet, and Warehouse (Glitches 20/33). Honey Droplist and
Capital One Shopping watchlists alert on wishlist price drops.

- Coupons page: https://www.amazon.com/coupons
- Tracker/extension alert options: https://harpa.ai/blog/best-amazon-price-trackers-and-drop-alerts

## 2. Community aggregators 🟢

- **Slickdeals** — the fastest human alert network; the $94.48 glitch was
  found and spread there in minutes. Create a Deal Alert on "amazon glitch"
  keywords. https://slickdeals.net · alerts: https://slickdeals.net/
- **Krazy Coupon Lady** — Amazon freebie/coupon roundups, tracks Shopper Panel
  etc. https://thekrazycouponlady.com/tips/money/amazon-shopper-panel
- **Price-error accounts** — @Pricerrors on X (268k followers), Instagram
  glitch reels, and paid groups (Frugal Season, Bandar's Bounties, EMoney,
  House of Resell UK) post glitches within minutes. https://x.com/Pricerrors · https://whop.com/blog/amazon-glitch-deals

## 3. Do Honey / Capital One still work on Amazon? 🟢

Yes, in a limited way: Amazon doesn't use checkout promo *codes* like most
stores, so Honey's code-testing rarely fires there — but Honey's **Droplist**
price-drop tracking and Capital One Shopping's **price comparison / watchlist**
features still work on Amazon product pages. Use them for tracking, not codes.
Stack order: Rakuten session → Capital One/Honey watch → card rewards at
checkout.

- Extension comparison incl. Amazon behavior: https://www.joinkudos.com/blog/rakuten-vs-honey-vs-capital-one-shopping---comparing-the-top-shopping-rewards-extensions
- Capital One Shopping on Amazon: https://capitaloneshopping.com
- Honey Droplist: https://harpa.ai/blog/best-amazon-price-trackers-and-drop-alerts

## 4. Rebate sites on Amazon 🟢/🟡

- **Rakuten** lists Amazon with category cashback (varies 1–10%, changes
  monthly; must click through Rakuten before adding to cart, and gift cards /
  some categories are excluded). https://www.rakuten.com
- **Mr Rebates / Great Canadian Rebates** — same model; GCR also rebates
  Prime Video subscriptions in Canada. https://www.greatcanadianrebates.ca/details/Amazon-Prime-Video
- Cashback is computed on the *post-coupon* price — a big Honey-style code can
  be worth more than the cashback; pick one. https://www.joinkudos.com/blog/rakuten-vs-honey-vs-capital-one-shopping---comparing-the-top-shopping-rewards-extensions

## 5. Combined stack (set up once)

Keepa/CamelCamelCamel alerts (Glitch 36) + Slickdeals deal alert + coupon-page
sweep + Rakuten click-through + Droplist watch on big-ticket wishes. Every
layer is free; each catches deals the others miss.
