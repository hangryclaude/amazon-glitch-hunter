# Glitch 18 — Prime Gaming free games & loot 🟢

**Category:** Free digital goods (Prime)
**Effort:** Low — claim on a schedule
**Risk:** None.

## How it works

Prime Gaming (now largely served through **Amazon Luna**) gives Prime
members free stuff every month:

- **Free PC games** — claim codes to redeem on Epic Games Store, GOG, etc.
  Games are **yours to keep forever** even if Prime lapses. Line-ups run
  2–10+ games monthly; past 2026 drops include DOOM, Mafia III, the Tomb
  Raider remasters, and XCOM: Chimera Squad.
- **In-game loot** — free skins, currency, and items for Fortnite, Roblox,
  Genshin-adjacent titles, mobile games, etc.
- Some months also include free **Luna cloud-streaming** channels.

## How to work it

- Bookmark the claims page and claim **everything every month**, even
  games you don't want — redemption codes don't expire with membership.
- Link your Epic/GOG accounts once; then claiming is one click.
- October 2026 gave **10 free games** (Polygon-documented) — event
  months are richer; check Prime Day / holiday drops.
