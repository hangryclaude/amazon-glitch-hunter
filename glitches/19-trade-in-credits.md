# Glitch 19 — Amazon Trade-In bonus credits 🟢

**Category:** Trade-in promo
**Effort:** Medium — ship an old device/book
**Risk:** None. Amazon's program.

## How it works

Amazon's Trade-In program pays an **Amazon gift card** for used devices,
phones, Echo/Fire gear, games, and books. On top of the base value,
Amazon regularly adds:

- **Bonus 20–25% off (or rebate toward) a new qualifying Amazon device**
  when you trade in an old one — these "bonus discount" promos recur
  monthly and around Prime Day.
- **Instant Payment** option on many items: gift card value lands
  immediately at trade-in creation, before they even receive the item.
- Book trade-ins sometimes carry bonus-gift-card promos during
  back-to-school windows.

## How to work it

- Time trades to **Prime Day / October Big Deal Days** — rebates stack
  with already-deep event pricing (Business Insider-documented strategy).
- Trade dead/scratched-but-booting devices: value is quoted **up front
  by condition**; "functional issues" tiers still pay.
- Combine: trade in old Echo → 25% bonus off new device → pay partly
  with glitch credits from Glitches 11/16.
