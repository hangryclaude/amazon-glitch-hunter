# Program Tests — live/dead verification (Oct 2026) 🔍

**Purpose:** Verify which cataloged Amazon programs are actually live and
working as of **October 15, 2026**, against official Amazon pages and 2026
reporting. Companion to the `glitches/` catalog.

**Method:** official-page checks (Amazon, Woot, aboutamazon) plus 2026
press/deal-blog reports. Each entry names the exact claim from the catalog,
then gives STATUS + evidence + source.

**Verdict:** 7 live · 2 changed · 1 dead

---

## Results

### Amazon Vine (Glitch 13) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** invitation-only review program, free products, no application.
- **STATUS: live** — official Vine page still up and still describes an
  invitation-only program selecting "the most insightful reviewers"; no
  application path, invite emails/popups only.
- Source: https://www.amazon.com/vine/about
- Source (2026 guide confirming invite-only, no application): https://aipinsiders.com/how-to-become-an-amazon-vine-reviewer-in-2026/

### Amazon First Reads (Glitch 17) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** ~10 free pre-release Kindle books monthly for Prime; two-book months.
- **STATUS: live** — October 2026 selection is up; Prime members get **two**
  free picks this month (non-Prime $1.99 each), picks drop the 1st, never
  repeat. October's two-free-books month is documented by multiple outlets.
- Source: https://www.amazon.com/firstreads
- Source: https://mashable.com/article/oct-1-amazon-first-reads-deal
- Source: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/books-and-authors/prime-reading-free-books-benefits (updated Sep 17, 2026)

### Prime Gaming (Glitch 18) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** monthly free PC games via Prime (served through Luna), keep forever.
- **STATUS: live** — October 2026 lineup is **10 free games** (DOOM on GOG,
  Five Nights at Freddy's: Into the Pit on Epic, XCOM: Chimera Squad, Ad
  Infinitum, Kiborg, etc.), staggered through the month.
- Source: https://www.polygon.com/amazon-gaming-luna-october-2026-doom-10-free-games/
- Source: https://gamerant.com/prime-gaming-free-games-list-october-2026/

### No-Rush shipping credits (Glitch 11) — 🟡 CHANGED

- **Catalog claim:** $1 digital credit per No-Rush order, reliably.
- **STATUS: changed** — the offer itself is still live (official No-Rush store
  page active: "discount immediately... or automatically apply a reward to
  your account"), but the guaranteed **$1 digital credit is no longer
  consistent** in 2026: it's frequently replaced by instant order discounts,
  account-targeted offers, or (with Amazon-branded cards) ~1% delayed-shipping
  rewards instead. Expect the *perk*, not the fixed dollar.
- Source: https://www.amazon.com/b?ie=UTF8&node=9433645011
- Source (reward-form drift): https://www.amazonforum.com/s/question/0D56Q0000CIZDZsSQP/no-rush-digital-rewards-why-not-offered-anymore?language=en_US
- Source (1%-with-Prime-card reports): https://sellersasksellers.com/t/digital-credits-are-gone-buyer-side-for-delayed-shipping/3096

### Amazon Fresh first-order promo (Glitch 15) — 🟡 CHANGED

- **Catalog claim:** fixed first-order codes (`GROCERY10` $10, `FRESH50`, $20-off-$100).
- **STATUS: changed** — the first-order *offer* still exists but there is no
  stable public code value as of Oct 2026; promos are now mostly targeted or
  item-scoped. Currently documented: **free delivery on first Fresh order
  $50+ with Prime**, plus item-level grocery promos ($10 off $30+ selected
  grocery, $10 off $35 General Mills, both Oct 2026). The Prime Access
  $5-off-$25 monthly grocery credit ran July–Sept 2026 and **expired Oct 1,
  2026**. Old codes `GROCERY10`/`FRESH50` are not verified current.
- Source (first-order free delivery, official): https://www.amazon.com/fmc/m/30007513?almBrandId=QW1hem9uIEZyZXNo
- Source ($10 off $30, Oct 2026): https://www.usatoday.com/deals/amazon
- Source (Prime Access credit, expired): https://www.amazon.com/promotion/psp/A27MIQ24WDQF5I

### Trade-in bonus promos (Glitch 19) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** 20–25% bonus off a new qualifying device with trade-in; recurs monthly/around Prime Day.
- **STATUS: live** — Trade-In store and bonus terms both active; official
  terms still grant a **20% discount** on a new qualifying Amazon device per
  accepted trade-in, with category/event boosts to **25%** (e.g. Kindle
  e-reader promos); Prime Day 2026 trade-in strategy documented by Business
  Insider.
- Source: https://www.amazon.com/l/9187220011
- Source (20% bonus terms): https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GT3YDPKTADB6LX2Z
- Source (Prime Day 2026): https://www.businessinsider.com/guides/deals/trade-in-your-amazon-device-for-prime-day-2026-6

### Amazon Haul (Glitch 27) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** sub-$20 storefront, free shipping at $25+, tiered 5%/$50 and 10%/$75 discounts, sub-$3 returnless.
- **STATUS: live** — Haul still running in Oct 2026 with the same mechanics:
  **$25 free-shipping threshold** (flat fee below), the 5%/10% tiers, and
  sub-$3 returnless refunds; best selection in the Amazon Shopping app.
- Source (current 2026 terms round-up): https://fabulesslyfrugal.com/amazon-haul-deals/
- Source: https://www.izoate.com/blog/what-is-amazon-haul-how-it-works-prices-safety-2026/

### Prime Try Before You Buy (Glitch 23) — 🔴 DEAD

- **Catalog claim:** already marked RIP in the catalog — ended Jan 31, 2025.
- **STATUS: dead (confirmed)** — ended for new orders **Jan 31, 2025** (final
  returns processed into spring 2025); 2026 status articles confirm it has not
  returned, with Amazon citing AI sizing/virtual try-on as the replacement.
  The catalog is correct to mark it dead.
- Source: https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/amazon-ends-prime-try-before-you-buy-service
- Source (2026 status: discontinued): https://unanswered.io/guide/amazon-try-before-you-buy-status

### Woot / Bag of Crap (Glitch 20) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** daily deals, Woot-Offs, Bag of Crap still a thing; Prime free shipping.
- **STATUS: live** — woot.com is active (© 2004–2026 footer, 7 categories),
  Woot-Offs still run, Prime members still get free standard shipping, and
  the **Bag of Crap still exists** — the current FAQ's return policy has an
  explicit "Bags of Crap: sorry, no returns" entry.
- Source: https://www.woot.com/
- Source (BoC + Woot-Offs, official FAQ): https://www.woot.com/faq

### Amazon Family diaper discount (Glitch 21) — 🟢 LIVE

- **Catalog claim:** up to 20% off diapers via Amazon Family + Subscribe & Save.
- **STATUS: live** — 2026 sources confirm Amazon Family (free with Prime)
  still provides **up to 20% off diapers** when ordered via Subscribe & Save
  (stacking with the S&S 5–15% tiers).
- Source: https://www.amazon.com/family
- Source (2026 confirmation): https://www.thriftynorthwestmom.com/benefits-amazon-family-save-diapers-baby-items/
- Source (S&S tiers + Family 20%, cites Amazon 2026): https://www.thebargainsisters.com/the-best-amazon-subscribe-and-save-deals/

## Honesty notes

- **Try Before You Buy is the only dead program** in this set — don't hunt it.
- The two **changed** programs (No-Rush credits, Fresh first-order) still
  exist but no longer match their catalog write-ups exactly: No-Rush rewards
  are variable instead of a fixed $1, and Fresh first-order value is
  targeted/rotating instead of stable public codes. Update Glitches 11 and
  15 accordingly if desired.
- Verification date: **Oct 15, 2026**. Program terms rotate monthly; re-check
  event months (Prime Day, October Big Deal Days) for boosted variants.
